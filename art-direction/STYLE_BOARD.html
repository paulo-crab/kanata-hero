Kanata Hero
Visual Style Bible

Overhead office adventure. Authentic 16-bit-era pixel craft: hard pixels, hand-placed clusters, expressive characters, open readable routes. The approved overhead concept leads; seven player screenshots support it.

Camera

High orthographic three-quarter overhead. No horizon, perspective convergence, or side-scrolling parallax.

Pixel craft

One uniform logical pixel grid. Hard edges and deliberate clusters. No blur, gradients, antialiasing, or filter-made pixels.

Detail budget

Give people and one district landmark the richest clusters. Keep ordinary floors, walls, and props reusable and calm.

Light

Warm upper-left highlights; deep blue-purple shadows. Selective dark contours and compact contact shadows.

Scale · decided 2026-10-01

16×16

Floor cell

One logical grid for every world asset.

16×24

Person frame

1 cell wide by 1.5 high, feet on a one-cell footprint. Expression lives in 48×48 portraits.

320×180

Logical view

20×11 cells. Whole-number scaling only: ×4 on 1366×768, ×6 on 1920×1080.

Chosen from the side-by-side test in scale-test/. The earlier 32×32 / 48×64 and 48×48 / 72×96 proposals are retired. Never mix pixel sizes.

Rules artists can check on every asset

Characters

Visible crown and shoulder tops; expressive face at 1×; asymmetric human stance; hair and clothing shaped by clusters, not texture noise.

Materials

Stone uses broad slabs; glass gets one stepped reflection; wood has light top/dark side; brass gets tiny warm glints; foliage grows in grouped masses.

Layering

Floor → rear walls/props → contact shadows → actors → front occluders → hard-edged local light → crisp DOM UI.

Outline

Deep blue-purple on overlap and underside. Break lit edges with local dark color. Avoid thick full black rings.

Texture

One coherent pixel size, few clusters on ordinary tiles, restrained dithering only on large transitions. No isolated noise.

Readability

Two-cell routes, exits, people, terminals, and task markers survive the keyboard inset. Focal garden stays bounded.
