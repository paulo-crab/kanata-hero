# Background office workers

**Status:** art approved by the director 2026-10-02. The pixel spec [BACKGROUND_WORKERS_SPEC.md](../../art-direction/cast/BACKGROUND_WORKERS_SPEC.md) is canonical where it differs from this brief, and [ART_HANDOFF.md](../../art-direction/ART_HANDOFF.md) indexes the files. Shared rules: [README](README.md).

## Identity

| | |
| --- | --- |
| What they are | Unnamed staff who populate the floors. They show Pace's control through synchronized motion, and its loosening when quests resolve. |
| Interaction | None stated. They are not quest givers and carry no coral conversation marker. |

Sources: `levels.md` levels 01, 06, 18, 19, 20 and the Orientation row; ART_BRIEF; concept 08.

## Visual rules

- Same 16×24 frame, anchor, and construction rules as the named cast. They live on the same grid.
- **Simpler than named characters**: they must never out-read the Engineer, NPCs, or interactables.
- **Assumption:** build them from a small set of shared bodies with palette swaps (the docs require a reusable kit and a tight art budget).
- Never violet. Avoid saturated teal, coral, or gold accents.
- Concept 08 shows workers seated at glass-room desks and standing at doors, in muted tops and dark trousers.

## Behavior and states

| Where / when | State | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orientation, level 01 | Two workers walk the **same tiny loop at the same moment**: an early hint of over-management | Level 01 |
| Orientation, before review | **Synchronized** worker loops | Orientation row, ART_BRIEF |
| Orientation, after level 06 | Workers **stop marching in sync**; loops break into **individual idle poses** and they relax | Level 06, ART_BRIEF |
| Night Shift, level 18 | A **coworker silhouette** appears behind the interior window for each repaired ticket | Level 18 |
| Night Shift, level 19 | Silhouettes start moving independently | Level 19 |
| Executive, level 20 | Named cast arrive as **silhouettes first**, then recognizable sprites | Level 20 |

### Silhouette treatment

**Assumption:** a "silhouette" is the same sprite filled with one or two ink tones (`#202337` `#343650`) and a lit-edge step where light allows. It keeps the shape and drops the detail. The same treatment serves for Ivo, Noor, Hal, Ada, and Mira's arrival in level 20.
