# Orientation: art needed beyond the current kit

Every prop below is placed in `map.json` with `art_gap: true` and declared in `art_gap_entries` (footprint, collision, layer) and `art_gap_state_sets`. The names are the agreed Orientation names from the level contract; the producer reconciles them with the kit team's atlas. Footprints are this team's decision so collision can be derived now: the kit team may change a footprint if the art needs it, and then the placements and `collision` rows change with it (`validate_levels.py` shows exactly which cells).

Two entries are **not** on the agreed list and are this team's additions: `wall_w_plain` and `garden_north_rim_open`.

Placement and offsets follow ART_HANDOFF section 3.6: the footprint origin sits at the placement cell, `offset` is visual only. State sets (`elevator`, `turnstile`, `clock_twin`, `conference_door`, `pinboard`, `garden_north_rim`) are declared in `map.json` under `art_gap_state_sets`; their state ids are the suffixes of the entry names.

## Notes for the kit team

- **Elevator.** Three states `closed`, `half`, `open` (120 ms each, like `records_door`); only `open` is walkable. It sits on the west wall at x0-1, y11-13 and the avatar arrives at (2,12). `wall_w_plain` is omitted on those three rows so the elevator's own collision governs; if the elevator art does not cover the wall face there, add a frame piece.
- **Records door.** The kit door `records_door` is used as is at x26-27, y5-7. `wall_e_plain` is omitted on those rows for the same reason; check that the door art covers the wall face behind it.
- **Turnstile.** Drawn as a pair 1 cell wide and 2 cells tall, closing a 2-cell passage at x19, y13-14. If the art is wider, change the footprint and the passage together.
- **Conference door.** 2 cells wide in the south glass wall of the clock room (x5-6, y6), opening from `closed` through `half` to `open`.
- **Wall-mounted pieces** (`pinboard_*`, 2x2 at x0-1, y8-9 on the west wall) carry collision `00` because the wall cells beneath already block.
- **Garden cut-through.** The kit's `garden` landmark `after` state opens the south rim and shows three flagstones. The level data opens column x12, y7-10 as a through route, so the north rim needs `garden_north_rim_open` (a 1x1 floor marking at (12,6)). If the north opening is not drawn, level 06 can still use the south opening only by changing the `g.garden-cutthrough` gate to cells (12,10) and (12,9); the shortcut then becomes a dead-end nook (the hidden seating nook in the levels.md revisit payoff).
- Entries reused from the kit: walls, windows, alcove, `records_door`, `lamp` (printer, desk, corridor and door lamps), planters, `sofa`, `side_table`, `printer`, `desk_a`, `desk_b`, `chair`, `bench`, `mail_counter`, `partition_1x1/2x1`, `route_inlay`, `records_mat`, the garden landmark, and Pace signage (`pace_wall_sign_2x1`, `pace_directory`, `pace_floor_arrow_e/n/w/s`).
